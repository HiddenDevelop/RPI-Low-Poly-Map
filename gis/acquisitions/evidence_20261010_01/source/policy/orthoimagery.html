      Viewing Orthoimagery

      There are many ways to view the program's orthoimagery. Perhaps the most direct is to use the interactive Discover GIS application at orthos.dhses.ny.gov. This application also allows you to download the imagery you're viewing in case you want to use it in other software.

       

       

       

      Web services are also available for use in GIS software or map applications.  (Check out our ShareGIS page if you're unfamiliar with data web services.)

      You can also download imagery through a table based presentation.

      Statewide Status Overview

      The New York Statewide Digital Orthoimagery Program has been producing orthoimagery since 2000. High-resolution orthoimagery is available statewide for viewing and downloading. The program's goal is to continue obtaining imagery for the entire State on a 4 to 5 year cycle.

      Next Flight

      At the end of each Summer, detailed planning for the next orthoimagery update begins. The plan incorporates information such as the status of the current update, the age of orthoimagery in different areas, and funding. Once a plan has been approved, outreach to the relevant counties begins and the new project area is released. The map below shows the imagery area planned for Spring 2027.

      click here to enlarge image

      Counties include: Allegany, Cayuga, Genesee, Jefferson, Lewis, Livingston, Monroe, Ontario, Orleans, Oswego, Seneca, Steuben, Tompkins, Wayne, Wyoming, and Yates
